Privacy Policy

EduSphere

This Privacy Policy explains how EduSphere ("the App"), operated by Ali Software Studio ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the App on a mobile device or through our related services.

EduSphere is a school management platform used by school principals, teachers and parents/guardians. By creating an account in, or using, the App you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Important: School accounts are created by the school administration (or by us on behalf of a school). We do not publish advertising, and we do not sell personal information to third parties.

We collect information that is necessary to run the school management service:

2. How Information Is Used

  • To provide, operate and maintain the App and your school's account.
  • To authenticate you (mobile number + password for Principal/Teacher, mobile number + PIN for Parents).
  • To let authorised users of the same school view and manage school records.
  • To send push notifications about attendance, homework, classwork, test and exam results, school notices and holidays.
  • To generate reports, result cards (DMC) and fee receipts as PDF files on your device.
  • To enforce licence/subscription status (active, trial, expired, suspended).
  • To respond to support requests sent by email or WhatsApp.
  • To secure the service, prevent misuse and fix bugs.

3. How Information Is Shared

School-level sharing only

Each school's data is isolated (multi-tenant). A principal, teacher or parent can only see data belonging to their own school — and a parent can only see their own child or children. No school can access another school's data.

  • Within your school: information is shared with the school staff and parents who are authorised for that record.
  • Service providers: we use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging) and Cloudflare R2 (file storage) to host and process data. These providers process data only on our instructions.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law, regulation, court order or to protect the rights and safety of users.
  • Business transfer: if the service is acquired or merged, data may be transferred under the same protections, and we will notify you.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Data Storage, Location & Retention

  • Data is stored on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and files on Cloudflare R2, which may be located outside Pakistan (including the United States and other Google/Cloudflare regions).
  • Data is kept for as long as your school's account is active, or as long as needed to provide the service.
  • When a school's licence is deleted, or you request deletion, we remove the school's records from our live databases within a reasonable period, subject to legal backup retention cycles.
  • Some information may persist in encrypted backups for a limited time before being permanently overwritten.

5. How We Protect Your Data

  • Role-based access control (Principal / Teacher / Parent) enforced in app and in database security rules.
  • Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Server-side security rules that prevent cross-school data access.
  • Passwords/PINs are stored as hashes, never in plain text.
  • Keystore-backed signing and restricted release builds.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we use industry-standard measures, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The App contains student records, which may relate to children under 13. Those records are entered and controlled by the school, not by the child. The App is intended for use by schools and parents/guardians, and we do not knowingly create accounts for children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child's personal information has been provided to us without proper consent, contact us and we will remove it promptly.

8. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access / correction: you can view and update your profile in the App, or ask your school administrator to correct student records.
  • Notifications: you can turn notifications on or off at any time in Android settings or in the App.
  • Deletion: you can request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us.
  • Withdrawal of consent: you may stop using the App and uninstall it at any time.
  • Language / theme: the App supports English and Urdu, and a dark mode — these preferences are stored on your device.
